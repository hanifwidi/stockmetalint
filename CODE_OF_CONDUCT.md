# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, and accept feedback gracefully. Unacceptable
behaviour includes harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, publishing others'
private information, and any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the maintainer (GitHub: `hanifwidi`) through GitHub's private
reporting features. All reports are reviewed promptly and confidentially. Maintainers may
remove, edit or reject contributions and may ban contributors who violate this code, following
the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
